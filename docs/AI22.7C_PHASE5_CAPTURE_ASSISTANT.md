## Objective

Live classroom guidance during capture — **guidance only, never blocks capture**.

## Signals (reuse quality pipeline)

- Lighting / blur (via `getImageQualityIndicator`)
- Estimated faces (session detectedFaces when available)
- Stability / distance / framing hints

## Example tips

- Move slightly left
- Move back
- Too dark
- Too blurry
- Camera shaking

## Architecture impact

- Pure presentation component over existing blur / quality utilities.
- Capture buttons remain enabled regardless of tip severity.
